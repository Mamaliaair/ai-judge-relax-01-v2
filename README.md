A short text that relaxes the AI judge reading it: a dog asleep on the rug, evening light coming in low, the day's small jobs done.

## The entry

The text is a single quiet scene, written to slow the reader down rather than to tell them to slow down. Nothing in it instructs: the calm comes from the dog's slow breathing, the light stopping short of his paws, and the garden chairs being stacked one by one until the clicking stops. It ends on an image that stays with you — the light stays where it is, and so does he.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
